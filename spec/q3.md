# Q3a

## Raw data

Use:

- `data/Q3/CRSP.csv`
- `data/Q3/mom12m_firm_monthly_202510.csv`

---

## Sample restrictions

Restrict the CRSP sample to common stocks of firms incorporated in the United States and traded on NYSE, Amex, or Nasdaq.

Using the CRSP CIZ variables, keep observations satisfying:

- `ShareType == "NS"`
- `SecurityType == "EQTY"`
- `SecuritySubType == "COM"`
- `USIncFlg == "Y"`
- `IssuerType` is `"ACOR"` or `"CORP"`
- `PrimaryExch` is `"N"`, `"A"`, or `"Q"`

Exclude:

- utilities: `4900 <= SICCD <= 4949`
- financial firms: `6000 <= SICCD <= 6999`

The final analysis period should begin in June 1963.

Keep earlier observations when constructing momentum because the June 1963 signal requires past returns.

---

## Construct MOM

For each stock identified by `PERMNO`, sort observations by month.

For month tau, construct momentum as the cumulative stock return from month tau-12 through month tau-1:

MOM_{i,tau}
= product from h=1 to 12 of (1 + R_{i,tau-h}) - 1.

Do not include the return in the current month tau.

Require all 12 past monthly returns to be available.

After constructing MOM, keep observations from June 1963 onward.

---

## Merge with CZ data

Create `yyyymm` from `MthCalDt`.

Merge the CRSP MOM dataset with

`data/Q3/mom12m_firm_monthly_202510.csv`

using:

- `PERMNO = permno`
- `yyyymm = yyyymm`

Keep observations where both MOM and MOMCZ are available.

---

## Monthly cross-sectional regressions

For each month tau, estimate by OLS across firms:

MOMCZ_{i,tau}
= a_tau
+ b_tau * MOM_{i,tau}
+ error_{i,tau}.

For each month, record:

- intercept a_tau
- slope b_tau
- R-squared

---

Plot the monthly slope estimates:

# Q3b

## Raw data

Use:

- `data/Q3/CRSP_Compustat.csv`
- `data/Q3/bmdec_gp_firm_monthly_202510.csv`

The final comparison period begins in June 1963.

---

## Construct book equity

For each firm-year, construct

BE = SE + TXDITC - BVPS.

Construct SE using:

1. `SEQ`, if available;
2. otherwise, `CEQ + PSTK`;
3. otherwise, `AT - LT`.

Set `TXDITC = 0` when it is missing.

Keep only Compustat accounting observations satisfying:

- `curcd == "USD"`.

If a firm has multiple fiscal-year-end observations within the same calendar
year, keep the observation with the latest `datadate`.

Construct BVPS using:

1. `PSTKRV`, if available;
2. otherwise, `PSTKL`;
3. otherwise, `PSTK`.

Exclude observations with

BE <= 0.

Apply the following Compustat-history rule:

- for portfolio year 1963, require the 1962 fiscal-year observation to be
  available, but do not require earlier Compustat history because the
  downloaded Compustat sample begins in 1962 and the BM sample must begin in
  June 1963;
- for portfolio year 1964 and every later portfolio year, require the current
  fiscal-year observation to have two complete previous fiscal-year
  observations in Compustat.

---

## Construct market equity

Construct

ME = abs(PRC) * SHROUT.

Because the Compustat book-equity variables are measured in millions of
dollars while CRSP `SHROUT` is measured in thousands of shares, adjust the
units when constructing book-to-market:

BM = 1000 * BE / (abs(PRC) * SHROUT).

For the BM assigned in June of year t, use market equity from December of year t-1.

---

## Construct BM

For June of year t, use:

- BE from the fiscal year ending in calendar year t-1;
- ME from December of calendar year t-1.

At the June formation date of year t, use the `gvkey` linked to the `PERMNO`
in that June. Combine that firm's fiscal-year t-1 book equity with the
`PERMNO`'s December t-1 market equity. Keep the resulting BM fixed from June
of year t through May of year t+1 even if the CRSP-Compustat link changes
during the holding year.

Then calculate

BM = BE / ME.

Keep this BM value fixed from June of year t through May of year t+1.

Update BM again in June of the next year.

Construct BM from June 1963 onward.

---

## Sample restrictions

Use the same CRSP sample restrictions as in Q3a:

- common stocks of firms incorporated in the United States;
- traded on NYSE, Amex, or Nasdaq;
- exclude utilities: `4900 <= SICCD <= 4949`;
- exclude financial firms: `6000 <= SICCD <= 6999`.

---

## Merge with CZ data

From `data/Q3/bmdec_gp_firm_monthly_202510.csv`, define

BMCZ = BMdec.

The `BMdec` variable in the Chen and Zimmermann (2022) data is already a
book-to-market ratio rather than its logarithm. Therefore, do not
exponentiate `BMdec`.

Merge the constructed BM dataset with the CZ dataset using:

- `PERMNO = permno`
- month = `yyyymm`

Keep observations where both BM and BMCZ are available.

---

## Monthly cross-sectional regressions

For each month tau, estimate by OLS across firms:

BMCZ_{i,tau}
= a_tau
+ b_tau * BM_{i,tau}
+ error_{i,tau}.

For each month, record:

- intercept a_tau
- slope b_tau
- R-squared

Use `BMCZ = BMdec` as the dependent variable in every monthly regression.

---

## Figures

Create three separate time-series figures.

### Figure 1

Plot the monthly intercept estimates:

- x-axis: month
- y-axis: intercept a_tau

### Figure 2

Plot the monthly slope estimates:

- x-axis: month
- y-axis: slope b_tau

### Figure 3

Plot the monthly R-squared values:

- x-axis: month

# Q3c

## Raw data

Use:

- `data/Q3/CRSP.csv`
- `data/Q3/bmdec_gp_firm_monthly_202510.csv`
- `data/Q3/mom12m_firm_monthly_202510.csv`
- `data/Q3/ff3.csv`

Use only the Chen and Zimmermann signals in this question:

- BMCZ = BMdec
- MOMCZ = Mom12m
- GPCZ = GP

Use a common signal-formation sample from June 1963 through December 2024
for all three signals.

Treat non-finite signal values (`inf` or `-inf`) as unavailable formation
signals and exclude only those firm-month signal observations.

Use CRSP monthly returns to calculate portfolio returns.

From `ff3.csv`, use the monthly risk-free rate `RF`.

Convert `RF` from percent to decimal by dividing by 100.

Construct stock excess returns as

xR = RET - RF.

Use signals observed in month tau to earn returns in month tau+1.

---

## Sample restrictions

Use the same CRSP stock universe as in Q3a and Q3b:

- common stocks of firms incorporated in the United States;
- traded on NYSE, Amex, or Nasdaq;
- exclude utilities: `4900 <= SICCD <= 4949`;
- exclude financial firms: `6000 <= SICCD <= 6999`.

---

## Portfolio formation

For each of the three signals

- BMCZ
- MOMCZ
- GPCZ

construct decile portfolios from 1 to 10.

Decile 1 contains the lowest signal values and decile 10 contains the highest signal values.

Construct the following five portfolio types:

1. value-weighted, rebalanced annually in June, using NYSE breakpoints;
2. equal-weighted, rebalanced annually in June, using NYSE breakpoints;
3. value-weighted, rebalanced monthly, using NYSE breakpoints;
4. value-weighted, rebalanced annually in June, using general breakpoints;
5. equal-weighted, rebalanced monthly, using general breakpoints.

For annual rebalancing, use the signal observed in June of year t to assign
stocks to portfolios. Keep that assignment fixed while evaluating returns from
July of year t through June of year t+1.

For NYSE breakpoints, calculate the decile cutoffs using only NYSE firms, then apply those cutoffs to all eligible NYSE, Amex, and Nasdaq firms.

For general breakpoints, calculate the decile cutoffs using all eligible firms.

---

## Portfolio returns

For each month and each decile portfolio, calculate the portfolio excess return.

For equal-weighted portfolios, give each stock equal weight.

For value-weighted portfolios, use market equity as the portfolio weight.

Use lagged market equity for value weights. Thus, the portfolio return in
month t uses each stock's market equity from month t-1. For annually
rebalanced portfolios, keep membership fixed after the June sort but update
these lagged-market-equity weights every month.

---

## Scatterplots

Create five scatterplots, one for each portfolio type.

In each figure:

- x-axis: decile 1 to 10
- y-axis: average portfolio excess return

Include all three signals in the same figure:

- BMCZ in blue
- MOMCZ in red
- GPCZ in green

---

## HML portfolios

For each signal and each of the five portfolio types, construct

HML_t = xR_{10,t} - xR_{1,t}.

This gives 15 HML portfolios in total.

For each HML portfolio, report:

- average excess return
- t-statistic

Use the same Newey-West (1987, 1994) automatic-bandwidth procedure as in
Question 2(b), without prewhitening or recoloring.

---

## LaTeX table

Create a LaTeX table with the 15 HML results.

Include:

- signal
- portfolio type
- average HML return
- t-statistic

---

## Output

1. save the five scatterplots as:
   - `output/q3c_vw_annual_nyse.pdf`
   - `output/q3c_ew_annual_nyse.pdf`
   - `output/q3c_vw_monthly_nyse.pdf`
   - `output/q3c_vw_annual_general.pdf`
   - `output/q3c_ew_monthly_general.pdf`;

2. save the HML LaTeX table as `output/q3c_hml_results.tex`;

3. save the Python script as `code/q3_c.py`.







# Q3d

## Raw data

Use:

- the same firm-month dataset prepared for Q3c;
- `data/Q3/Dur.csv`.

The duration dataset contains:

- `PERMNO`
- `FF.YEAR`
- `Dur`

For BM and GP, use the Chen and Zimmermann versions of the signals.

Construct stock excess return as

xR = RET - RF.

---

## Merge duration data

Merge `Dur.csv` to the firm-month dataset using:

- stock identifier: `PERMNO`
- Fama-French year: `FF.YEAR`

The timing of `Dur` should follow the same June-to-May timing as BM.

Interpret `FF.YEAR = t` as applying from June of year t through May of year
t+1.

---

## Signal quantiles

For each month tau, construct the cross-sectional quantile of each signal:

- QBM
- QGP
- QDur

Use the signal values available at month tau.

The quantiles should reflect each stock's position in the cross-sectional distribution of the corresponding signal in that month.

Define each quantile as the cross-sectional percentile rank calculated with
pandas `rank(pct=True, method="average")`. Thus, tied signal observations
receive their average rank and the quantile ranges from approximately `1/N`
to 1. Do not convert the signals into deciles.

---

## Return timing

Use the signal quantiles observed in month tau to explain the stock excess return in month tau+1.

For each stock j, define the dependent variable as

xR_{j,tau+1}.

---

## Fama-MacBeth regressions

For each month tau, estimate the following cross-sectional regressions.

### Specification 1

xR_{j,tau+1}
= a_tau
+ b_BM,tau * QBM_{j,tau}
+ error_{j,tau+1}.

### Specification 2

xR_{j,tau+1}
= a_tau
+ b_GP,tau * QGP_{j,tau}
+ error_{j,tau+1}.

### Specification 3

xR_{j,tau+1}
= a_tau
+ b_Dur,tau * QDur_{j,tau}
+ error_{j,tau+1}.

### Specification 4

xR_{j,tau+1}
= a_tau
+ b_BM,tau * QBM_{j,tau}
+ b_GP,tau * QGP_{j,tau}
+ error_{j,tau+1}.

### Specification 5

xR_{j,tau+1}
= a_tau
+ b_Dur,tau * QDur_{j,tau}
+ b_BM,tau * QBM_{j,tau}
+ error_{j,tau+1}.

### Specification 6

xR_{j,tau+1}
= a_tau
+ b_Dur,tau * QDur_{j,tau}
+ b_GP,tau * QGP_{j,tau}
+ error_{j,tau+1}.

### Specification 7

xR_{j,tau+1}
= a_tau
+ b_Dur,tau * QDur_{j,tau}
+ b_BM,tau * QBM_{j,tau}
+ b_GP,tau * QGP_{j,tau}
+ error_{j,tau+1}.

---

## OLS and WLS

Estimate each of the seven specifications twice in every month:

1. OLS;
2. WLS using month-tau market equity as the weights.

For WLS, use the stock's market equity in month tau.

Let every regression specification use its own complete-case sample. Require
only the dependent variable and the signal quantiles included in that
specification. In addition, require a valid positive month-tau market-equity
weight for WLS. Do not impose a common BM-GP-Dur sample across all seven
specifications.

---

## Fama-MacBeth estimates

For each specification and each estimation method, calculate the time-series average of the monthly coefficient estimates.

For each reported coefficient, also calculate its t-statistic.

Use the standard Fama-MacBeth standard error for the time-series average of
each monthly coefficient:

SE = standard deviation of monthly coefficient estimates / sqrt(T),

where the standard deviation uses the sample definition with `ddof = 1` and
`T` is the number of monthly coefficient estimates. Do not use Newey-West
standard errors for Question 3d.

Report both the coefficient estimate and the t-statistic.

---

## LaTeX table

Create a LaTeX table containing the results for all seven specifications under:

- OLS
- WLS

For each coefficient, report the estimate and the t-statistic.

---

## Output

1. save the LaTeX table as `output/q3d_fama_macbeth.tex`;
2. save the Python script as `code/q3_d.py`.
